We started with a question: why is the most essential part of a camping trip — the light — still stuck in 2005?

Most campers have two options: a harsh LED flashlight that feels like a interrogation room, or a bulky gas lantern that's heavy, loud, and a fire hazard in the wrong hands.

Neither of them make you want to stay outside longer.

Our team spent 14 months rethinking what outdoor light should feel like. Not brighter — better. We engineered a lantern that runs 40 hours on a single charge, weighs less than a water bottle, and casts a warm glow that dims from gentle candlelight to full table illumination. No fuel. No cords. No compromises.

The response from early users surprised even us. It wasn't just backpackers reaching out — it was glampers, tailgaters, van-lifers, and families who simply wanted their backyard patio to feel like a retreat.

The outdoor industry is shifting from "survival gear" to "experience gear." People aren't just tolerating the outdoors anymore — they're curating it. And the products that win will be the ones that respect both function and feeling.

We're proud to be part of that shift. And we're just getting started.
